# Antiques by Anna — Web Analytics & Digital Strategy Analysis

**Tools:** Google Analytics, Google Search Console

---

## Purpose of the Project

Analyzed the digital performance of Antiques by Anna, a boutique
vintage jewelry e-commerce site, using real Google Analytics data.
The goal was to identify friction points in the customer journey and
deliver actionable recommendations to reduce bounce rates, improve
retention, and increase conversions.

All insights were evaluated through the lens of a target persona:
Sasha — a high-end, style-savvy vintage collector based in NYC.

---

## Key Findings from Google Analytics

| Metric | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| Total Views | 3,477 |
| Active Users | 374 |
| Bounce Rate | 39.95% (high from Instagram traffic) |
| Avg. Engagement Time | 2m 56s |
| Returning Users | Low — majority are new visitors |
| Checkout Completion | 100% abandonment after Step 1 |
| Top Traffic Source | Direct (32%), Organic Social (34%) |

---

## Problems Identified

**1. High Bounce Rate — Poor Landing Page Engagement**
* Users land on generic product grids with no story, CTA, or emotional hook
* No curated themes or welcome messaging to guide the experience
* Feels like a catalog, not a boutique vintage destination

**2. Weak User Retention**
* No email signup prompt or loyalty incentive on the site
* "Favorite" feature exists but is buried and not tied to an account
* Cart abandonment email is delayed and triggers in limited cases
* No personalized messaging or lifestyle content to bring users back

**3. Broken Checkout Funnel**
* 100% cart abandonment after Step 1 (Begin Checkout)
* 88% of checkouts initiated on mobile — no mobile optimization
* No smooth handoff from cart to payment

---

## Recommendations

### Short-Term Fixes
1. Redesign landing page with editorial-style visuals and compelling CTAs
   ("Shop the Collection", "Learn More About This Era")
2. Add story-driven product descriptions highlighting era, origin, and styling tips
3. Launch an email newsletter — inexpensive, direct, and versatile
4. Add conversion tags in GA: "Email Signup", "Purchase Complete", "Referral"
5. Fix checkout flow — ensure all funnel steps track properly in GA

### Long-Term Strategy
1. Launch "Collectors' Circle" — a premium loyalty/membership program with
   early access, monthly webinars, reward points, and a private community
2. Add a "Flea Market Collection" section with rotating spotlight items,
   countdown timers, and limited-edition tags to drive urgency
3. Target introductory-price items (under $200) to thrift/flea market audiences
   and integrate with local NYC community events
